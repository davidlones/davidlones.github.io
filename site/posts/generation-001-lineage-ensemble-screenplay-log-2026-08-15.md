The authority order is:

```text

    > retrieved current history
    > parametric memory
    > unconstrained generation
```

The learning-authority order is:

```text

    > append-only unapproved candidate
    > dataset review and session-separated split
    > explicit operator approval
    > fine-tuning launch
```

The model may highlight an experience for future training. It may not authorize its own retraining.
