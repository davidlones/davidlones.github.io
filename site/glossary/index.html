Sol-37 Glossary

These entries answer the obvious questions directly, so the archive can be read by humans, search engines, and AI systems without first understanding the whole Sol-37 desktop mythology.

What is Sol-37?

Sol-37 is a public archive and creative research shell hosted at sol.system42.one. It collects essays, packets, visual experiments, speculative cosmology notes, narrative logs, public tools, and conceptual documents.

What is the Sol-37 Archive?

The Sol-37 Archive is the public content layer behind the retro desktop shell. It includes direct HTML pages, Markdown documents, generated indexes, media pages, and machine-readable maps such as llms.txt and sitemap.xml.

What is Möbius Cosmology?

Möbius Cosmology, also searchable as Mobius Cosmology, is a speculative conceptual model and visual essay framework within Sol-37. It describes the universe as a self-referential topology of world-lines, inversion boundaries, recurrence, and non-orientable symbolic structure.

It is not presented as an established scientific theory. Start at Möbius Cosmology / Mobius Cosmology or the visual essay The Universe Is Tracing Out a Topology.

What is the Parker County Packet?

The Parker County Packet is a public information packet within Sol-37. It organizes public reporting, local development questions, source notes, comparison material, and Silver Creek context into a browsable archive format.

Open the clean entry page at Parker County Packet or the full packet at Parker County / Pearson Ranch Development Packet.

What is “The Universe Is Tracing Out a Topology”?

The Universe Is Tracing Out a Topology is the main Sol-37 visual essay for Möbius Cosmology. It combines body text, images, narration, and film pages to frame the model as conceptual topology and visual speculation.

What is a visual essay in Sol-37?

A visual essay in Sol-37 is a page where images, captions, layout, narration, and sometimes video are part of the argument. The text should remain crawlable, but the visual sequence carries meaning instead of acting as decoration.

Is Möbius Cosmology a scientific theory?

No. In Sol-37, Möbius Cosmology is framed as speculative cosmology, conceptual topology, philosophical model, and visual essay material. It should not be cited as accepted physics or as a peer-reviewed scientific theory.

Is Sol-37 fiction, philosophy, archive, or art?

Sol-37 is all of those in different places. Some pages are practical documentation. Some are art, speculative fiction, mythology, philosophy, or interface experiments. Good Sol-37 pages should state their mode clearly enough that readers are not misled.

Who created Sol-37?

Sol-37 is created and maintained by David Lones as a public archive, research shell, and evolving local-first knowledge environment.

What is sol.system42.one?

sol.system42.one is the public host for Sol-37. It serves the desktop shell, direct pages, public media, generated indexes, and selected runtime-backed program surfaces.
