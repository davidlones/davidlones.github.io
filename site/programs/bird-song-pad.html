Why these shapes

The earlier clip suggested brief broadband chirps and chatter bursts instead of long tonal whistles. This synth keeps chirps short, bright, and irregular for that reason.

What “translation” means here

These labels are human convenience handles for timing, energy, and clustering. They are not a claim that Barn Swallows map cleanly onto literal English phrases.

Use in the field

Keep playback brief, low volume, and infrequent. Repeated alarm-like output can stress birds or distort their normal behavior around nests.

Operating Note

Shell integration

This page publishes a lightweight audio bridge into the parent Sol shell, so the desktop orb can react to synthetic chirp energy the same way it reacts to embedded page audio.
